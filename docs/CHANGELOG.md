## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Multi-tab browsing with a tab switcher; tabs and the active tab survive restarts.
- Address bar that opens addresses or searches with a configurable engine.
- Back, forward, reload, stop and share.
- History with search, and bookmarks with edit and remove.
- Private tabs that leave no history and keep no cookies.
- Downloads through the platform transfer UI.
- Settings: home page, search engine, desktop site mode, clearing history, cookies and cache.
- Cover showing the current tab's title and icon, with a new-tab action.
- Finnish translation.
